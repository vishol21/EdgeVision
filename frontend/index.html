How these results work

YOLO locates people and vehicles in sampled frames. ByteTrack links detections into tracks. A track is not a verified unique person: occlusion can split it. Review moments flag sustained rapid movement, upright-to-wide person posture changes, possible vehicle contact with abrupt motion change, or configured scene rules. These image-space cues can be wrong and cannot confirm robbery, accidents or safety.
